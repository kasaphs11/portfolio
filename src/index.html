Museum Installation

Project In The Museum

Two interactive games, built in Unreal Engine in collaboration with Professor Ioannis Fountos, are installed inside the GENERAL ARCHIVES OF GREECE - HISTORICAL ARCHIVES - MUSEUM OF EPIRUS and form an active part of the museum itself.

They run on touchscreen kiosks in the exhibition “Benefaction and Benefactors, 18th–20th century”, turning historical engravings and maps into playable experiences, with the menus, UI and gameplay logic built in Blueprints.

Built with Unreal Engine Blueprints Touchscreen Kiosks Live In The Museum
Soufari Sarai, Kastro, D. Filosofou & Glykiton 1, Ioannina 452 21
01 Unreal Engine · Interactive Game

The Lake

An interactive game built around a historical engraving of the castle and lake of Ioannina, playable by visitors directly on the museum's touchscreen kiosk.

Websites

02 Client projects
La Crema · Coffee & Patisserie
01 Café & Patisserie · Veria, Greece

La Crema

A warm, editorial website for a patisserie running since 1976. The homepage opens on a real-time 3D scene, a branded coffee cup and bougatsa on marble, that follows the visitor through the coffee, bougatsa, patisserie and events chapters.

  • Real-time 3D scene in Three.js with custom GLB models
  • Scroll-driven storytelling across the menu chapters
  • Local SEO with structured data for the café
  • Responsive, mobile-first layout and navigation

FireZone by Pasvantidis
02 Fire Safety Equipment · Veria, Greece

FireZone

A cinematic website for a fire-safety company with over 30 years of experience. A Blender-rendered hero with live fire and reflections animates as the visitor scrolls, leading into services, the extinguisher catalogue and contact details.

  • Scroll-driven hero animation rendered frame by frame in Blender
  • Canvas-animated fire, reflections and branded intro loader
  • Dark / light theme that remembers the visitor's choice
  • Lenis smooth scrolling that respects reduced motion
